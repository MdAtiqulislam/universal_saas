# DOC-21: Glossary & Domain Terminology

**Version:** 1.0 | **Status:** APPROVED | **Date:** 2026-08-27
**Purpose:** Standardize domain language across the project.
**Owner:** Technical Writer

## Core Platform

- **Organization / Tenant:** A customer entity using the SaaS platform. Data is isolated per Organization.
- **Platform Admin:** Internal employee managing the SaaS infrastructure.
- **User:** A human identity with access to the system.
- **Member:** A User's specific association/membership with an Organization.
- **Role:** A collection of Permissions.
- **Permission:** Granular access right (format `resource.action`).

## Business Domain

- **Product / Item / Material:** Configurable goods managed by the system.
- **Inventory / Stock:** Quantities of Products held in Warehouses.
- **Warehouse:** Physical locations holding Stock.
- **Purchase:** Process of acquiring goods from Suppliers.
- **Supplier:** External vendor providing goods.
- **Sales:** Process of selling goods to Customers.
- **Customer:** Entity purchasing goods.
- **Production:** Process of converting raw materials to finished goods.
- **Shipment:** Delivery of goods.
