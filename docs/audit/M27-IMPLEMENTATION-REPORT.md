# Milestone M27 — Implementation & Verification Report

## Executive Summary

Milestone M27 (**Procurement & Purchase Order Management Foundation**) has been successfully implemented, verified, and integrated into the Universal Business Operations SaaS platform.

The module provides a production-grade procurement execution workflow that converts material supply recommendations generated by M26 MRP into an end-to-end operational pipeline:
$$\text{MRP Planned Order (M26)} \to \text{Purchase Requisition} \to \text{Purchase Order} \to \text{Approval \& Budget Check (M23)} \to \text{Supplier Confirmation} \to \text{Goods Receipt (M09/M19/M12)} \to \text{AP Matching Reference (M13)}$$

## Core Capabilities & Module Integrations

1. **MRP Planned Order Integration (M26)**:
   - Planned Orders with `action = PURCHASE` and `status = SUGGESTED` are converted to Purchase Requisitions.
   - Prevents double-conversion, preserving immutable planning snapshots.
2. **Purchase Requisition Lifecycle**:
   - `DRAFT` $\to$ `SUBMITTED` $\to$ `APPROVED` $\to$ `CONVERTED` $\to$ `CANCELLED` / `REJECTED`.
3. **Purchase Order Management & Supplier Pricing**:
   - Manages quantities, discounts, taxes, and shipping terms with exact Decimal precision.
   - Preserves historical order pricing upon creation.
4. **Approval & Budget Control (M23)**:
   - Integrates with `BudgetControlService` enforcing `CHECK_ONLY`, `WARN`, and `BLOCK` policies before PO approval.
5. **Supplier Confirmation**:
   - Captures supplier acknowledgment, reference codes, confirmed delivery dates, and notes.
6. **Goods Receipts & Atomic Inventory/Costing Integration (M09/M19/M12)**:
   - Atomic transaction enforces remaining quantity ceilings (prevents over-receipt).
   - Generates stock `RECEIPT` movements, updates `InventoryBalance`, creates M19 `InventoryCostLayer`, and captures batch/serial tracking data.
   - Transitions PO status to `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED` or `RECEIVED`.
7. **Purchase Returns**:
   - Reverse physical inventory (`ISSUE`), decrement balance, and provide source references for M13 Accounts Payable debit notes.
8. **Procurement Reporting**:
   - 6 detailed reports: PO Summary, Open Orders, Supplier Purchases, Requisitions, Goods Receipts, and Procurement Spend.
