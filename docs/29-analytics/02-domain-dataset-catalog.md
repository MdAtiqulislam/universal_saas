# Domain Dataset Catalog

## Overview

The platform exposes 12 authoritative domain datasets via the `AnalyticsDefinitionRegistry`:

1. **`sales.revenue`**: Sales order totals, discounts, taxes, and net margins.
2. **`sales.orders`**: Order volume, payment status, and fulfillment throughput.
3. **`inventory.stock`**: Stock quantities on hand, reserved quantities, and valuation.
4. **`inventory.turnover`**: Ledger movements, inventory velocity, and adjustments.
5. **`warehouse.throughput`**: Picking, packing, put-away tasks, and duration.
6. **`quality.defects`**: Inspection results, defect rates, and vendor dispositions.
7. **`returns.rate`**: RMA volumes, reasons, resolutions, and refunds.
8. **`service.tickets`**: Ticket volumes, response times, SLA adherence, and CSAT.
9. **`finance.invoice`**: Invoicing, receivables/payables aging, and outstanding balances.
10. **`crm.customer`**: Customer lifecycle stages, account tiers, and lifetime value.
11. **`workflow.execution`**: Workflow automation execution rates, step counts, and latencies.
12. **`notifications.delivery`**: Omnichannel message volumes, read rates, and latencies.
