# 13. Operational Commercial Reporting & Metrics

## 10 Operational Reports

The platform provides 10 key operational commercial reports generated by `BillingReportsService`:

1. **Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR)**: Normalizes active subscriptions by billing interval (monthly base vs. yearly / 12) with breakdown by plan.
2. **Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR)**: $\text{MRR} \times 12$ projection.
3. **Subscription Status Summary**: Active, Trialing, Past Due, Paused, Cancelled distribution.
4. **New Subscriptions Acquisition**: Time-series grouping of newly established subscriptions.
5. **Customer Churn Analysis**: Cancelled subscriptions vs. active base with calculated churn rate %.
6. **Upgrade & Downgrade Movement**: Audit trail of plan changes and proration movements.
7. **Accounts Receivable & Invoice Aging**: Bucketed into Current (0–30 days), 31–60 days, 61–90 days, and 90+ days.
8. **Payment Gateway Reliability**: Success rate percentage, attempt counts, and settled volumes.
9. **Usage & Quota Telemetry**: Aggregated consumption across metered metrics.
10. **Revenue by Plan & Tier**: Historical invoice revenue grouped by product tier.
