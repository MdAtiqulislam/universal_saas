# ADR-153: Scheduled Report Delivery Via M36 Jobs and M43 Channels

## Status

Accepted

## Context

Automated distribution of business intelligence reports is an essential enterprise capability. Rather than building a separate scheduler or email sender, the platform must reuse existing core infrastructure: M36 `JobService` (for background scheduling, retries, bounded concurrency) and M43 `NotificationsService` (for omnichannel messaging, quiet hours, and delivery tracking).

## Decision

1. **M36 Job Registration**: `ReportSchedulingService` registers the `execute_scheduled_report` job handler with M36 `JobService`.
2. **Frequency & Cron**: Schedules support `DAILY`, `WEEKLY`, `MONTHLY`, and `CUSTOM` (cron) frequencies, updating `lastRunAt` and calculating deterministic `nextRunAt`.
3. **M43 Notification Delivery**: Generated reports (or export attachments) are dispatched via M43 `NotificationsService`, respecting recipient channel preferences and quiet hours.
4. **Bounded Execution**: Exports generated by schedules are capped at 10,000 rows to prevent background worker memory exhaustion.

## Consequences

### Positive

- Zero duplication of scheduling or messaging infrastructure.
- Complete auditability and retry resilience inherited from M36.
- Full compliance with user notification preferences inherited from M43.
