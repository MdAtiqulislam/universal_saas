# Milestone M27 — Procurement & Purchase Order Management Foundation

## Overview

Milestone M27 establishes a production-grade Procurement and Purchase Order Management engine for the Universal Business Operations SaaS platform. It seamlessly links supply planning outputs generated by Milestone M26 MRP into an execution workflow spanning Purchase Requisitions, Purchase Orders, Budget Control (M23), Supplier Confirmations, Goods Receipts, Inventory Balances & Movements (M09), Inventory Cost Layers (M19), and Three-Way Invoice Matching readiness (M13).

```text
MRP Planned Order (M26)
        ↓
Purchase Requisition (PR-000001)
        ↓
Purchase Order (PO-000001)
        ↓
Budget Control & Approval (M23)
        ↓
Supplier Confirmation & Delivery Date
        ↓
Goods Receipt & Atomic Stock Update (M09/M19/M12)
        ↓
Accounts Payable Matching Ready (M13)
```

---

## 1. Purchase Requisitions

### 1.1 Lifecycle States

- `DRAFT`: Initial creation and editing of requested items, required dates, and suggested suppliers.
- `SUBMITTED`: Locked for review by department/procurement authorizers.
- `APPROVED`: Authorized for procurement; eligible for conversion to PO.
- `CONVERTED`: Converted into a formal Purchase Order.
- `CANCELLED`: Cancelled prior to conversion.
- `REJECTED`: Rejected during authorization.

### 1.2 M26 Planned Order Conversion

- Planned Orders with `action = PURCHASE` and `status = SUGGESTED` are converted into Purchase Requisitions via `POST /api/v1/procurement/planned-orders/:plannedOrderId/convert`.
- The source Planned Order is marked `CONVERTED` and stored in `sourcePlannedOrderId`, ensuring full auditability and preventing duplicate conversion.

---

## 2. Purchase Orders & Approvals

### 2.1 Lifecycle States

`DRAFT` $\to$ `SUBMITTED` $\to$ `APPROVED` $\to$ `SENT` $\to$ `ACKNOWLEDGED` $\to$ `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED` $\to$ `RECEIVED` $\to$ `CLOSED` (or `CANCELLED` / `VOIDED`).

### 2.2 Budget Control Integration (M23)

Before a Purchase Order can be approved, the total amount is evaluated against active budget policies:

- **`CHECK_ONLY`**: Logs budget check result and permits approval.
- **`WARN`**: Emits `PURCHASE_ORDER_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` warning event, logs warning, and permits approval.
- **`BLOCK`**: Emits `PURCHASE_ORDER_BUDGET_EXCEEDED` and immediately rejects approval with `BadRequestException`.

## 3. Goods Receipts & Inventory Integration

### 3.1 Receiving Workflow

- Triggered via `POST /api/v1/procurement/purchase-orders/:id/receive`.
- Atomically executes within a database transaction:
  1. Validates that received quantities do not exceed remaining quantities (prevents over-receipt).
  2. Generates immutable Goods Receipt (`GR-000001`).
  3. Creates M09 `StockMovement` of type `RECEIPT`.
  4. Updates M09 `InventoryBalance` (`quantityOnHand`).
  5. Creates M19 `InventoryCostLayer` for lot/FIFO tracking.
  6. Captures batch and individual serial numbers where required.
  7. Updates PO line `receivedQuantity` and `remainingQuantity`.
  8. Transitions PO status to `RECEIVED` (if all lines fulfilled) or `PARTIALLY_RECEIVED`.

---

## 4. Purchase Returns

### 4.1 Return Workflow

- Triggered via `POST /api/v1/procurement/returns` and `POST /api/v1/procurement/returns/:id/post`.
- References source Goods Receipt and Purchase Order.
- Enforces invariant: $\text{Return Quantity} \le \text{Received Quantity}$.
- Atomic posting reverses stock movement (`ISSUE`) and decrements `InventoryBalance`.

---

## 5. Procurement Reports

1. **Purchase Order Summary**: Status distribution and spend totals (`totalSpend`, `openSpend`, `receivedSpend`).
2. **Open Purchase Orders**: Line-level breakdown of open commitments and expected receipt dates.
3. **Supplier Purchase Report**: Vendor-specific metrics (total purchase value, order count, received value, outstanding value).
4. **Purchase Requisition Report**: Status counts and requisition details.
5. **Goods Receipt Report**: Delivery tracking with unit and line costs.
6. **Procurement Spend Report**: Flexible aggregation grouped by supplier, item, category, location, or month.
